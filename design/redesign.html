01 — Studio

The workbench

Three columns, nothing floating, nothing wasted. The conversation in the middle, the project's machinery in a column that never closes. It is the densest of the five and the one that scales to a project with forty tools and a long todo list.

  • Right column that holds context, model, mode, tools and the live event feed
  • Projects become initials in a 66px rail — the list is never a scroll away
  • Tool calls sit inline in the prose, the way a terminal shows them
  • One accent for what is alive, one for what is done

02 — Deck

The conversation as a deck

The opposite of the workbench: soft, quiet, generous. Every turn is a card on a lit ground, the projects are a shelf of pills across the top, and the composer is a single floating capsule that never gets in the way. It reads like a letter, and it feels like nothing at all.

  • Cards on a lit ground with two slow-drifting coloured lights behind them
  • Projects become a shelf of pills instead of a list
  • One floating capsule for the composer, blurred over everything
  • Tool calls fold into the card as a quiet strip

03 — Command

The control room

Not a chat window that happens to hold one agent — a board of every agent, each one live. The transcript is one card among many, and the card you are not reading keeps working while you read another. This is the design for running six projects at once and knowing which one needs you.

  • One card per project, each with a live miniature of its transcript
  • A sparkline of activity per card, drawn in as it loads
  • Working cards are swept by a light; the board reads as alive
  • The whole board is a top-level view — a project opens into a pane

04 — Timeline

The reading view

A quiet, printed page. The agent's work is a spine of events down the left — a message, a thought, a tool call, a question — and the prose runs beside it. It is the only one of the five that would be comfortable to read for an hour, and the only one that is light by default.

  • Every event is a node on a spine — nothing is hidden, nothing is a widget
  • Paper, warm white, a comfortable measure and real leading
  • Thinking and tool calls are inline, in the order they happened
  • The composer is a single line at the foot of the page

05 — Glass

The console

Everything recedes until you need it. The history sits behind the prompt as blurred glass and sharpens as you reach for it; the controls are a bar of light at the bottom of the screen. It is the most cinematic of the five, and the one that would make a live turn feel like something happening rather than something being reported.

  • A single question at the centre of the screen, at 34pt
  • History is blurred glass that sharpens on approach
  • Two slow drifting lights and a film of grain behind it all
  • The composer is a luminous capsule; the bar of controls is barely there
